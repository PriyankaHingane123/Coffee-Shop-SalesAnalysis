# Coffee Shope Sales Analysis

# Business Problem-

Coffee shop owners need clear insights into sales performance to imporve revenue and operational efficiency. The goal of this project is to analyze sales
data to identify

top-performing products,revenue trends, and sales opportuities.

# Dataset Description-
The dataset contains daily coffee shop sales data, including:

1. Product details
2. Quantity sold
3. Revenue Information
4. Store-level sales records

# Tools & Technologies Used-
  1. SQL / MySQL
  2. Power Bi

# Steps Performed-
1. Performed data cleaning and vaildation
2. Used SQL queries for:
   a. data extraction
   b. Joins
   c. Aggregations
   d. Group By and Case statements
3. Identified key sales KPIs such as revenue, quantity sold, and top products
4. Created interactive Power BI  dashboards
5. Analyzed trends across products and stores

# Key Insights-

1. A small set of products contributed to the majority of revenue
2. Certain stores consistently outperformed others.
3. Sales peaks were observed  during specific time periods.
4. Identify 15-20% potential improvement opportunities in overall sales

# Dashboard/ Visual Output-

1. Interactive Power BI dashboards were built to visualize:
2. Total revenue
3. Product-wise comparison
4. Store-wise comparison
5. Sales trends over time

# Business Recommendations-
Based on the analysis and insights dervied from the coffee shop sales data, the following actionable business recommendations can be made:
1. Focus on High-Performing Products:
Prioritize inventory, promotions, and visibility for top-selling products that contribute the majority of revenue. Ensuring consistent availability of these
products can help maximize overall sales.

2. Optimize Underperforming Products:
Review low-performing products and evaluate pricing, placement, or demand, Consider improving marketing strategies or discontinuing products with
consistently poor performance.

3.Leverage High-Performing stores and analyze their practices (Location,Product mix , or sales timing). Apply these sucessful strategies across
underperforming stores to improve overall performance.

4.Improve Sales During Peak Time Periods:
Increase staff availability and stock levels during identified peak sales periods to avoid lost sales opportunities and improve customer experience.

5. Target Low-Sales Time Slot With Promotions:
Introduce Discounts,combo offer, or loyalty rewards during off-peak hours to drive addtional customer traffic and improve revenue consistency.

6. Invertory Planning Demand Forcasting :
Use Historical sales trend to forcast demand and optimize inventory levels, reducing overstocking and minimizing product wastage.

7. Data-Driven Pricing Strategy :
Analyze price sensitivity for key products and adjust pricing strategies to imporve profit margins without negatively impacting demand.

8. Continuous Performance Monitoring :
Regularly track KPIs such as total revenue,product-wise sales,store performance using Power BI Dashborads to support ongoing data driven decision.

# Conclusion
This analysis help coffee shop oweners make data-driven decision related to inventory planing, pricing strategies, and sales optimization.
